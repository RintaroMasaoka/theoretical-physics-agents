## Selection Rationale

### Foundational papers (★★★)

**#1 (0804.4527)** — Moore-Ran-Wen original proposal.
Source: direct search for "Hopf insulator Moore Ran Wen".
Abstract: "For the simplest case of two bands, these 'Hopf insulators' are characterized by a topological invariant as in quantum Hall states and Z_2 topological insulators, but instead of a Chern number or parity, the underlying invariant is the Hopf invariant that classifies maps from the 3-sphere to the 2-sphere." Also includes "a numerical study of the surface states of this model."

**#2 (1307.7206)** — Deng-Wang-Shen-Duan lattice model with arbitrary Hopf index and surface state analysis.
Source: direct search for "Hopf insulator surface states".
Abstract describes constructing "tight-binding model Hamiltonians which realize all kinds of Hopf insulators with arbitrary integer Hopf index" and verifying "robust surface states even under random perturbations lacking additional symmetries beyond the fundamental charge conservation."

**#3 (2501.05376)** — Correlated Hopf insulators: directly connects Hopf insulator QBT to QBT in Luttinger semimetals.
Source: search for "Hopf insulator surface states quadratic band touching".
Abstract: "At the topological quantum critical point separating a Hopf insulating phase from a trivial insulator, a quadratic band-touching point is realized at a high-symmetry point in the Brillouin zone, exemplifying an emergent Berry dipole." Also: "we highlight the interconnectedness of the effective descriptions of correlated Hopf insulators, two-dimensional quadratic band-touching semimetals, and Luttinger semimetals."

**#4 (1910.10717)** — Surface Berry curvature and bulk-boundary correspondence for Hopf insulators.
Source: search for "Hopf insulator surface states".
Abstract: "equal-energy bands with opposite Chern invariants can be spatially separated -- onto opposite facets of a finite crystalline Hopf insulator." Surface states "arise as non-chiral, Schrodinger-type modes on the domain wall of a generalized Weyl equation -- describing a pair of opposite-chirality Weyl fermions acting as a dipolar source of Berry curvature."

### Methods and background (★★☆)

**#5 (2208.02972)** — Nonlinear Hall effects at Berry-dipole transitions between Hopf phases.
Source: search for "Hopf insulator Berry dipole".
Abstract: "The critical points between two rotation-invariant Hopf insulator phases with different Hopf invariants...are uniquely marked by a quantized Berry dipole." Relevant for understanding the QBT at the Hopf transition.

**#6 (2404.10049)** — Berry-dipole semimetals from the Hopf map; surface Fermi arcs and quadratic dispersion near band nodes.
Source: search for "Hopf insulator Berry dipole topological critical point quadratic band touching".
Abstract describes "quantized Berry dipoles at band degeneracies" in a "two-band model based on the Hopf map" with "quadratic energy dispersion" near band nodes.

Source: search for "Hopf insulator Moore Ran Wen".

**#9 (2410.04021)** — Unconventional topological phase transition requiring both wave functions and gapless band structure.
Source: search for "Hopf insulator 2023 2024 2025".
Abstract: "the change of the Hopf invariant requires the information of wave functions as well as the gapless band structure simultaneously."

**#10 (2111.09365)** — Crystalline Hopf insulators: returning Thouless pump invariant, anomalous surface angular momentum.
Source: citation from search for "Alexandradinata Hopf insulator".
Abstract: "a new topological invariant which quantifies a 2pi-quantized change in the Berry-Zak phase...we refer to this new invariant as a returning Thouless pump (RTP)." Notes that "the RTP and Hopf invariants are delicate."

**#11 (1604.02840)** — Hopf-Chern insulators and the Hopf superconductor; gapless surface modes.
Source: search for "Hopf map homotopy classification topological insulator".
Abstract: "new three-dimensional topological phases of two-band models using the Pontryagin-Thom construction...hybrids of layered Chern insulators and the Hopf insulator."

**#12 (2303.13078)** — Real Hopf insulator: two independent Hopf invariants in real-valued Hamiltonians.
Source: search for "Hopf insulator 2023 2024 2025".
Abstract: "3D real-valued Hamiltonians with two occupied and two unoccupied bands...characterized by two independent Hopf invariants." Surface states have "nonzero Chern numbers in a PT-symmetric manner."

**#13 (2408.03908)** — Disorder effects on the Chern-Hopf insulator; stability and reentrant behavior.
Source: search for "Hopf insulator 2023 2024 2025".
Abstract: "The Chern-Hopf insulator is an unconventional three-dimensional topological insulator with a bulk gap and gapless boundary states without protection from global discrete symmetries...the disorder initially enhances the topological phase before driving it across an insulator-diffusive metal transition."

### QBT instabilities and interaction-driven phases (new entries from scout)

**#23 (0905.0907)** — Seminal paper on interaction instabilities at quadratic band crossing.
Source: search for "Sun Yao Fradkin Kivelson quadratic band touching checkerboard lattice topological".
Abstract: "a QBCP exists and is topologically stable for a Berry flux ±2π" when the point symmetry group has fourfold or sixfold rotational symmetry, and is "marginally unstable to arbitrarily weak short-range repulsive interactions." Four resulting ordered states: "quantum anomalous Hall phase, quantum spin Hall phase, nematic phase, and nematic-spin-nematic phase."

**#24 (1404.5721)** — Topological Mott insulator from QBT via Abrikosov non-Fermi liquid fixed point collision.
Source: search for "quadratic band touching topological insulator interaction instability".
Abstract: "a three dimensional electronic system with the Fermi level at the quadratic band touching point such as HgTe could be unstable with respect to the spontaneous formation of the (topological) Mott insulator at arbitrary weak long range Coulomb interaction" through "a collision between Abrikosov's non-Fermi liquid fixed point and a quantum critical fixed point."

**#25 (1403.4957)** — Weak-coupling RG analysis of interaction-driven QAH and QSH at QBT.
Source: search for "interaction-driven topological insulator spontaneous quantum anomalous Hall quadratic band crossing".
Abstract: "topological insulating phases driven by interactions can be realized without the need for spin-orbit coupling or large intersite interaction in a two-dimensional system of spin-1/2 fermions with a single pair of parabolically touching bands." "A quantum anomalous Hall phase is realized for Hubbard interaction, while a quantum spin Hall phase is favored for longer-ranged interactions."

**#26 (1503.04242)** — Nematic quantum criticality at 3D QBT.
Source: search for "Abrikosov non-Fermi liquid quadratic band touching three-dimensional".
Abstract: "field theory for tensorial nematic order parameter coupled to gapless fermions at the quadratic band touching point in three spatial dimensions." Identifies "a quantum critical point marking the transition from semimetal to a nematic Mott insulator phase."

**#27 (1611.05904)** — Anisotropy effects in 3D Luttinger semimetals with QBT and Coulomb interactions.
Source: search for "quadratic band touching nematic instability pyrochlore iridate Luttinger semimetal".
Abstract: "the intriguing role played by spatial anisotropy in three-dimensional Luttinger semimetals featuring quadratic band touching and long-range Coulomb interactions." For strong anisotropy, "the system exhibits an Abrikosov non-Fermi liquid ground state" and "quantum phase transitions into phases with nemagnetic orders -- higher-rank tensor orders that break time-reversal symmetry."

**#28 (1805.01101)** — Numerical phase diagram of QBT on checkerboard lattice.
Source: search for "interaction-driven topological insulator spontaneous quantum anomalous Hall quadratic band crossing 2D".
Abstract: "the instability of the QBT towards an interaction-driven spontaneous quantum anomalous Hall (QAH) effect" and "a symmetry-broken bond-ordered critical phase sandwiched in between the QAH and CDW phases, which splits the QBT into two Dirac points driven by interaction."

**#29 (1808.03560)** — Topological Mott insulators near QBT beyond half-filling.
Source: search for "quadratic band touching topological insulator interaction instability".
Abstract: "instability to topological insulating phases" near QBT "even away from half-filling."

**#30 (1811.12415)** — Global phase diagram of interacting spin-3/2 fermions in Luttinger semimetals.
Source: search for "Luttinger model quadratic band touching interaction-driven phases".
Abstract: "electronic interactions on gapless spin-3/2 excitations" in Luttinger semimetals map out "competing ordered states including rotational symmetry breaking nematic insulators and time reversal symmetry breaking magnetic orders." "Superconducting pairings can emerge even from repulsive interactions."

**#31 (2208.08455)** — Time-reversal-symmetric analog of the Hopf insulator.
Source: search for "Hopf invariant delicate topology returning Thouless pump".
Abstract: "a time-reversal-symmetric analog of the Hopf insulator" that is "the first known nonmagnetic delicate topological insulator incorporating spin-orbit coupling." Surface Z2 topology stabilized by "particle-hole and spatial inversion symmetries."

**#32 (2305.17189)** — Ubiquitous nematic Dirac semimetal from interacting QBT.
